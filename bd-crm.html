ASPIRe Phase Breakdown
A — Align
Establish strategic alignment between BD ambitions and organisational goals. Define OKRs, clarify mission/vision, confirm target markets. Customer-focused BRM principles.
OUTCOME: Strategy document · Agreed OKRs · Target segment map

OUTCOME: Market analysis · Value proposition · Competitive positioning
P — Plan
Build the BD roadmap. Define target account list (A/B/C tiers). Set up pipeline structure with weighted revenue. Event and communications planning.
OUTCOME: BD Roadmap · Pipeline tool · Contact list · Events calendar
I — Implement
Execute outreach. Manage warm/hot/top customer follow-up cadences. Submit proposals. Manage stakeholder relationships via Relationship Impact Matrix.
OUTCOME: Outreach logs · Proposals submitted · Meetings held
R — Review
Weekly OKR check-ins. RAG health scoring. Pipeline weighting review. Leading vs lagging indicator analysis. Healthcheck against BD best practices.
OUTCOME: RAG dashboard · OKR scores · Win/loss analysis
E — Evolve
Improve BD COE capability. Update methodology based on learnings. Invest in tools (GTMhub/OKR platform). Develop team skills. Iterate the ASPIRe cycle.
OUTCOME: Updated methodology · Skills development plan · Tool improvements

OKR Methodology for BD

OKRs (Objectives & Key Results) provide the BD team with a clear, measurable framework to drive outcomes rather than outputs. BD OKRs cascade from organisational strategy through team to individual level.

OKR Hierarchy
Org OKRs
Enterprise-wide objectives aligned to firm strategy. Include leading and lagging indicators.
↓
Team OKRs
BD team objectives supporting org-level goals. Cross-functional alignment with marketing and delivery.
↓
Individual OKRs
Personal objectives for BD managers, KAMs, and account directors. Tied to team OKRs.

OKR Best Practice — BD Application

OKR Writing Principles (DAINT)
D — Directional: Is it ambitious?
A — Achievable: Is it realistic?
I — Inspiring: Does it motivate?
N — Notable: Will it matter?
T — Time-bound: Has a deadline?
Key Results Checklist
  • Specific and time-bound — no vague language
  • Measurable and verifiable — binary or scored
  • Aggressive yet realistic (70% = success, not failure)
  • 3–5 KRs per Objective maximum (MECE principle)
  • Shift from lagging to leading indicators where possible
  • Weekly confidence check-in on each KR (0–100%)
  • Avoid dependency chains (domino effect)
  • Separate outcome OKRs from BAU/business-as-usual

Follow-Up Strategy by Contact Tier

Each contact category requires a different engagement cadence. These strategies are drawn from the BD Best Practices workbook and follow the 70/30 rule (70% value-add, 30% selling).

Top Customers
4× per year
  1. Strategic review — goals check-in, how to help
  2. Invite to sponsored event or conference
  3. Mid/Q3 check — same as above
  4. Send referral, holiday/year-end event invite
Hot Prospects
Consistently Until Closed
  1. Follow up immediately with thank you note
  2. Set next meeting and confirm next steps
  3. Send email with agreed action steps
  4. Add value — article, invite to educational event
70/30 Rule: 70% value-add, 30% selling
Warm Leads
6× per year
  1. Handwritten thank you note for their time
  2. Connect on LinkedIn
  3. Invite to chamber or community event
  4. Send a referral
  5. Set up another sales call
  6. Comment on their social media
Likely to Buy
2× per year
  1. Make a sales call once annually
  2. Invite to community event or network where they are
  3. Annual holiday card or newsletter
Stay visible without pressure. Long-game strategy.
Strategic Frameworks
Applied Frameworks
PRINCE2, Business Model Canvas, HCD, Double Diamond, JTBD, Lean Six Sigma, OKR, BTM, CPMAI and more
BMC
Business Model Canvas
Maps the nine building blocks of your BD value proposition: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships, and cost structure. Used to validate go-to-market strategy for each account tier.
StrategyValue PropSegments
P2
PRINCE2
Applied to BD proposal management and engagement delivery. Seven themes (Business Case, Organisation, Quality, Plans, Risk, Change, Progress) map directly to BD lifecycle stages. Particularly relevant to HL Plan delivery and RFP response governance.
GovernanceProposalsDelivery
HCD
Human-Centred Design
Puts the client at the centre of BD proposition design. Applied via the Double Diamond process: Discover client pain, Define the problem, Develop the solution, Deliver the pitch. Empathy mapping and JTBD frame the problem space for each account.
EmpathyDesignClient-Led
DD
Double Diamond
Design Council framework: two diverge/converge diamonds. First diamond — discover and define the right BD problem. Second diamond — develop and deliver the right solution. Prevents premature convergence on solutions before client needs are fully understood.
DiscoveryProblem SpaceSolution Design
JTBD
Jobs-to-be-Done
Reframes the BD conversation from "what do you sell" to "what job is the client hiring you to do?" Three job types: functional (execute the task), emotional (feel confident), social (be seen as a good decision-maker). Sharpens value proposition messaging.
Value PropClient InsightInnovation
LSS
Lean Six Sigma
Applied to BD process improvement. DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control) for eliminating waste in the proposal cycle. Lean thinking reduces the BD overhead — fewer but higher-quality pursuits. Win rate improvement as the key metric.
ProcessDMAICEfficiency
OKR
Objectives & Key Results
Core BD performance framework. Cascades from org → team → individual. Differentiates committed OKRs (must achieve) from aspirational OKRs (stretch targets). Weekly check-ins. CFR cadence (Conversations, Feedback, Recognition) sustains accountability.
PerformanceAlignmentCadence
BTM
Business Transformation Methodology
Applied when BD opportunity involves large-scale change. Provides structure for business case, stakeholder engagement, benefits realisation, and change management. Pairs with PRINCE2 for end-to-end programme governance on major accounts.
TransformationChange MgmtBenefits
CPMAI
PMI Cognitive Project Mgmt for AI
Framework for BD opportunities involving AI/ML/data capabilities. Six phases: Understand, Collect, Prepare, Analyse, Visualise, and Operationalise AI insights. Critical for pitching AI-enabled transformation services to FSI clients.
AI/MLDataPMI
HOBA
House of Business Architecture
Enterprise architecture approach for business transformation. Maps the "house" of business capabilities, processes, information, and organisation to deliver outcomes. Used in discovery phase to understand client AS-IS state and design future-state proposals.
ArchitectureEnterpriseBlueprinting
UJEA
User Journey & Experience Architecture
Maps the client's buying journey from first awareness through to contracted engagement. Identifies touchpoints, emotion curves, and drop-off risks. Informs BD communication design and ensures the buyer experience is as smooth as the end solution.

15 Strategies to Position Yourself as an Expert

These strategies build thought leadership and trust with target accounts over time. Consistency is more important than intensity. Combine digital, event, and direct approaches for maximum reach.

Digital Presence
  • LinkedIn Profile — Professional photo, authoritative description, 5+ testimonials
  • Blog — Write about client questions and areas of interest. Solve problems.
  • Video Content — Build YouTube/LinkedIn presence. Post organically.
  • Guest Blogging — Target accounts with larger audiences. Follow, promote, then ask.
  • SEO — Know what your client base searches for. Ensure site reflects it.
Thought Leadership
  • Webinars — Host deep-topic sessions. Record and share. Build email list.
  • Industry Magazines — Contact editors. Have article ready before asking.
  • Book / Long-form — Publishing positions you as definitive expert in your space.
  • HARO — Help A Reporter Out. Get quoted in industry publications.
  • Content is King — Quality > quantity. Answer client questions directly.
Network & Relationships
  • Speaking — Workshops, conferences, panels. Get on stage with your audience.
  • Podcasts / Radio — Be a guest. Build and share interviews.
  • Alliances — Comment on experts' blogs. Create joint projects. Share content.
  • Testimonials — Video, written, online. Social proof is critical to trust.
  • Networking Events — Invest time to connect and learn from your field's leaders.

Six Skills of Strategic Thinking for BD Leaders

1. Anticipate
Foresee changes at the periphery. Embrace market uncertainty as a source of advantage, not threat. Monitor emerging trends both inside and outside your industry. Adapt before you must.
2. Challenge
Challenge conventional wisdom. Examine sources of uncertainty. Reframe problems from multiple angles to understand root causes. Seek diverse views — see multiple sides before forming a position.
3. Interpret
Connect multiple data points in new and insightful ways. Use analytic reasoning AND seasoned intuition. Test multiple working hypotheses before converging on conclusions. Find the signal in the noise.
4. Decide
Show courage to set a distinctive strategic direction. Make tough choices with incomplete information. Balance long-term investment with short-term pressure. Speed AND rigour — not one at the expense of the other.
5. Align
Engage stakeholders. Understand change readiness. Manage differences and create buy-in. Rally the team around a compelling vision. Ensure capabilities, resources, commitments, and accountability metrics are in place.
6. Learn
Continuously reflect on successes and failures. Learn from interactions with customers, partners, and market segments. Conduct ongoing after-action debriefs. Don't fear experiments — they're the lifeblood of innovation.

User Guide — BD Intelligence Platform

Getting Started
Step 1: Review the Dashboard
The Dashboard gives you an executive overview of pipeline health, ASPIRe cycle progress, and target accounts. KPI cards update dynamically from your stored data.
Step 2: Add Your Prospects
Go to Pipeline & CRM → Outreach Tracker. Use the form at the bottom to add real prospects. Data is automatically saved to localStorage and persists across sessions.
Step 3: Update Contact Statuses
Navigate to Contacts → Target Accounts. Click on any row to edit status. Add new contacts using the "+ Add Contact" button in the page header.
Step 4: Complete the Playbook Sections
In BD Playbook → Value Proposition, save your firm's value proposition and SEEDS/WEEDS/NEEDS analysis. These are stored and auto-loaded on return.
Step 5: Use the Fee Estimator
In Resources → Rate Card, enter expected days per role to calculate engagement fees at standard and discounted (20%) rates. Update these figures for each proposal.
Toolbar Buttons
↓ JSON — Export to JSON
Downloads a .json file containing all current app state including prospects, contacts, saved texts, and configuration.
↓ PDF — PDF Export
Opens the browser print dialog with print-optimised styles. Save as PDF to export the current page view.
✉ Email — Email Results
Opens your default mail client with a pre-populated pipeline summary in the email body. Edit recipients before sending.
↺ Reset — Reset to Defaults
⚠️ Clears ALL localStorage data and reloads the app with default sample data. This action is irreversible — export JSON first if you want to preserve your data.
